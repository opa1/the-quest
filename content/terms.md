## 1. What The Quest Is

The Quest is a bounty board. Posters publish missions with an ADA reward attached. Hunters claim those missions, do the work, and submit proof. When a poster approves a submission, the reward is released to the hunter's Cardano wallet and the completion is recorded on-chain.

The Quest is a venue for that exchange. We are not a party to the agreement between a poster and a hunter, not an employer, and not an escrow agent in any regulated sense.

Rewards are paid in ADA to the wallet linked to your account. XP is awarded alongside and contributes to your public rank.
